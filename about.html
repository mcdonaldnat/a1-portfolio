Hi, I'm Natalie!

I’m a UC Irvine Game Design and Interactive Media student with a minor in Informatics, who is passionate about Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), UX/UI Design, accessibility, and creating user-centric digital experiences. My academic projects have given me hands-on experience translating complex user needs into actionable design solutions. I utilize user research, accessibility principles, wireframing, prototyping, and usability testing to bring users a painless and smooth experience.

With experience in Figma and a technical foundation in Python, JavaScript, Java, and Git/GitHub, I’m driven to create intuitive user-centered designs. I am seeking roles that contribute to UI/UX, Interaction Design, and HCI projects. Feel free to reach out to me, I would love to connect!

My thoughts on Accessibility

I truly believe that accesibility helps every user. A good design is an accesibile design. I strive to create designs that are intutive and accesibile for all users. This portfolio itself was made in accordance to WCAG 2.2 Level AA standards.
